Home Affairs Identity Verification (VerifyNow)

What it does:
This process is the connection to VerifyNow, an external provider that checks a South African ID number directly against Department of Home Affairs records, compares a selfie to the photo Home Affairs holds on file, and can check whether a person is physically present (liveness) rather than a photo or video being replayed.

Why it matters:
The organisation does not have its own direct link into Home Affairs' identity records, so VerifyNow acts as the trusted middleman that makes real government-backed identity verification possible. This is the strongest and most authoritative identity check available in the whole system - it isn't just checking a document photo, it's checking against the official government record.

How it works:
Three separate capabilities are available: verifying an ID number against Home Affairs records, matching a selfie against the photo Home Affairs holds (used when there's no other reference image to compare against), and a passive liveness check. Provider errors are surfaced clearly rather than hidden, and in the shared sandbox testing environment, the face-match and liveness checks share a short cooldown window, so they can't both be called back-to-back without one of them failing - which is why liveness is handled separately elsewhere in production.
